          History and memory

          This prototype doesn't save conversations or remember anything between sessions. A conversation exists only in the server's memory until you end it, leave it idle for an hour, or the server restarts.

          Your data

          There are no accounts yet, so there's no account to delete. Messages and voice recordings are sent to OpenAI to generate replies. Kinora doesn't keep recordings, and asks OpenAI not to store conversations for later retrieval.
